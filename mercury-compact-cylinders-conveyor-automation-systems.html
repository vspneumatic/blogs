Understanding the Role of Pneumatics in Conveyor Automation

Industrial conveyor systems transport raw materials, components and finished products between processing stations. During transportation, products often need to stop, change direction or move into specific positions.

Pneumatic systems perform these operations through compressed air and mechanical movement. Directional control valves regulate airflow, while actuators convert air pressure into linear motion.

When integrated with sensors and automated control systems, these components help maintain coordinated material movement throughout production facilities.

Major Applications of Compact Cylinders in Conveyor Systems

Compact pneumatic actuators perform several important functions in automated material handling equipment.

Product Stopping

Pneumatic actuators can operate stopper mechanisms that temporarily prevent products from moving forward. This allows inspection, processing or assembly operations to take place.

Product Sorting

Automated sorting systems separate products according to their dimensions, weight or inspection results. Pneumatic mechanisms help redirect lightweight products towards designated conveyor sections.

Material Transfer

Compact actuators can operate transfer mechanisms that move products between adjacent processing stations.

Product Alignment

Pneumatic positioning devices help align products before labelling, scanning, inspection or packaging.

Product Separation

Conveyor systems can use pneumatic mechanisms to maintain appropriate spacing between individual products before subsequent processing stages.

Technical Factors Affecting Conveyor Cylinder Performance

Selecting a pneumatic actuator for conveyor equipment requires careful technical evaluation.

Before purchasing components from Mercury Compact Cylinder Dealers, machine manufacturers should consider several important factors.

Operating Force

The actuator must generate sufficient force to operate the connected stopper, sorting device or transfer mechanism.

Stroke Length

The required travel distance should match the intended mechanical operation without interfering with surrounding equipment.

Conveyor Speed

Actuator response time must align with conveyor movement and product arrival frequency.

Mounting Position

Correct mounting arrangements help maintain mechanical stability and minimise unwanted lateral forces.

Product Weight

Heavier products may require reinforced mechanisms, external guides or purpose-designed conveyor stoppers to withstand impact loads.

Integrating Compact Cylinders with Sensors and PLCs

Automated conveyor systems often use photoelectric sensors, proximity sensors and programmable logic controllers to manage product movement.

A sensor detects an approaching product and sends a signal to the control system. The controller activates a directional control valve, which operates the pneumatic actuator.

Once the required mechanical operation is complete, the control system initiates the next stage.

This coordination allows manufacturers to automate repetitive material handling operations while maintaining consistent production sequences.

Compatible magnetic cylinder configurations may also support piston position detection.

How to Reduce Conveyor System Downtime

Unexpected interruptions in conveyor operations can affect multiple production stages. Preventive maintenance helps industrial businesses identify developing mechanical and pneumatic problems.

Maintenance teams should follow these practices:

  • Inspect pneumatic connections for air leakage.

  • Maintain appropriate compressed air filtration.

  • Check actuator mounting and mechanical alignment.

  • Monitor product sorting and stopping mechanisms.

  • Inspect sensors and directional control valves.

  • Avoid excessive impact loads on piston rods.

Regular inspections can help maintain consistent equipment performance. Always isolate machinery and release stored pneumatic energy before conducting maintenance.
